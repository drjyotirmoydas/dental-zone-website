A tooth advised for extraction, saved

Case No. 01: Upper front tooth (21). The previous root canal treatment had failed, with pus and swelling at the root. Several doctors had advised extraction, but the patient wished to keep the natural tooth. In three visits, without surgery, the tooth was saved through root canal re-treatment and an MTA apical barrier.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a serial?

Fill in the form above and send it on WhatsApp, or call directly. Dhaka chamber: 01728-885550; Madaripur and Planet Hospital: 01973-427777.

Why is a crown (cap) needed after root canal treatment?

After root canal treatment a tooth becomes relatively brittle, especially a back tooth. A crown protects it from fracture and restores chewing function.

What tests are needed before a dental implant?

Usually a CBCT (3D X-ray) is taken to assess the quantity and quality of the bone. The implant size and position are planned from it. General health, such as whether diabetes is controlled, is also considered.

Can adults have braces?

Yes. Adults with healthy teeth and gums can have braces. The length of treatment depends on the condition of the teeth.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Pulpotomy, pulpectomy, fillings and other necessary treatment of milk teeth are provided for children.

Is root canal treatment painful?

No. The area is fully numbed with local anaesthesia before treatment, so pain is usually not felt during the procedure. Mild discomfort may last a few days afterwards and settles with the prescribed medicine.

I was told elsewhere that my tooth must be extracted. Can it still be saved?

In many cases, yes. If a previous root canal has failed, re-treatment, removal of a broken file or sealing the root end with MTA can save the tooth. The doctor will advise after reviewing the X-ray.

What types of crowns are available?

Metal, metal-ceramic and metal-free (such as zirconia) crowns are offered according to need and budget. For front teeth, natural-looking metal-free crowns usually give the best result.

Braces or veneers to close gaps between teeth?

It depends on the size of the gap and the position of the teeth. Braces are suitable when teeth are protruding or crooked; for small gaps, composite or veneers are a quick alternative. The right method is advised after examination.

How much does treatment cost?

Cost depends on the type of treatment, the number of teeth and the materials used. An estimate is given in advance with the treatment plan after examination.
